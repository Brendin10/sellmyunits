The DSCR Calculator

A financed buyer can't offer more than their lender will lend plus the cash they're putting down. This works out the debt service coverage ratio on a property, the biggest loan that ratio supports, and the ceiling on what a buyer can actually pay you. Apartments, rental houses and self-storage are underwritten differently, so each has its own tab.

Two formulas, and which one applies to you

Lenders don't calculate DSCR the same way on every property. The line is at five units.

One to four units

Residential DSCR: gross rent ÷ PITIA. Principal, interest, taxes, insurance and association dues. No vacancy deduction, no repairs, no management fee. It's a generous formula, which is why a house can clear a 1.20 ratio on paper and still lose money in a month with a turnover.

Five units and up

Commercial: NOI ÷ annual debt service. Vacancy and every operating expense come out first, then the lender compares what's left to principal and interest only. Taxes and insurance already came out inside NOI, so they don't appear again in the debt service.

Self-storage

Commercial, same NOI formula, but usually a higher bar — often 1.25 to 1.35 — and frequently a 25-year amortisation instead of 30. A shorter schedule means a bigger payment and a lower ratio on identical income, so check it before you assume.

Why a seller should care

It's the ceiling on the offer

Most buyers of 2+ unit property borrow. Whatever your building is "worth," a financed buyer's number is their loan plus their cash — and the ratio caps the loan. Raise the NOI and you raise the loan they can get.

It tells you which lever matters

If the ratio is what's binding, more down payment doesn't help — income does. If the LTV is binding, the income is fine and the buyer just needs more cash. The calculator says which one you're looking at.

Rates move your price

Slide the interest rate and watch the maximum price move. That's not a market opinion, it's arithmetic — and it's a large part of why the same building is worth different amounts in different years.
