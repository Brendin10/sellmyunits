Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Forecast Plus ("we," "us") operates this website to connect people interested in buying, selling, or valuing multifamily properties with independent real estate professionals, lenders, and investors. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you provide: name, email, phone number, property address, and answers you give in our forms.
  • Automatic information: pages visited, referring site, campaign parameters (such as UTM tags), device and browser type, collected through cookies and similar technologies, including analytics and advertising tools.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request and connect you with a relevant professional or investor.
  • To contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry, as you consented to.
  • To measure and improve our website and advertising.

How we share it

We share the information you submit with independent third-party real estate agents, brokers, lenders, and/or investors who may contact you about your request. We may receive compensation from these partners. They use your information under their own privacy practices. We may also share information with service providers that help us run the site (such as form processing and hosting), and when required by law.

Texts and calls

If you consent, we and our partners may contact you using automated technology. Consent is not required to buy anything. You can opt out of texts at any time by replying STOP, and you can ask us to stop calling you.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing forecastplusllc@gmail.com. You can limit cookies through your browser settings.

Security & retention

We use reasonable safeguards and keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law.

Children

This site is not intended for people under 18.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here.
