Questions

Can I sell with tenants in place?

Yes. Most multifamily properties sell with tenants in place, and leases generally transfer to the new owner.

What if the building needs work?

Investors often look for value-add properties. Selling as-is is a common option.

Can I sell more than one property at once?

Yes. We work with portfolios, whether that's several single-family rentals, a few small buildings, or a mix. They can be sold as one package or one at a time, whichever nets you more.
