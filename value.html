Questions

Is this an appraisal?

No. It's an informal market estimate from a real estate professional, not a licensed appraisal. Lenders require their own appraisal.

Why do rents matter so much?

Income-producing properties are often valued on their net operating income, so rents and expenses drive value more than square footage does.

Will I be pressured to sell?

No. Many owners just want to know where they stand.
