For contractors & crews · IN · MI · WI

We sell the work. You do the work. We pay you.

We quote cleanouts and unit turnovers across Indiana, Michigan and Wisconsin, and we need good crews to take them on. You're not buying leads and you're not chasing homeowners — the job is already sold, scoped and scheduled when it reaches you.

How it works

1

You apply

Tell us your trades, your area and your capacity. Takes about three minutes.

2

We check you out

Insurance, any licence the work needs, and a couple of references. Nothing onerous, but we do actually check.

3

We send you jobs

Already quoted and sold, with a written scope and a date. You say yes or no to each one.

4

You invoice us

You work for us, not the property owner. You invoice us and we pay you.

Who we're looking for

  • Cleanout and junk removal crews — a truck, a team and a dump account
  • Turnover trades — painters, flooring, cleaners, general repairs
  • EPA Lead-Safe Certified firms — we refer all pre-1978 paint work out, and we need people to refer it to
  • Licensed trades — plumbing, electrical, HVAC, roofing, siding, grounds
  • Crews who answer the phone and say so when a date is going to slip
  • Anywhere in Indiana, Michigan or Wisconsin — we're busiest in northern Indiana and southwest Michigan

What we'll ask for

  • A certificate of insurance — general liability, sent by your insurer rather than forwarded by you
  • Any licence the work actually requires — which varies: Indiana has no statewide general contractor licence, Michigan requires a Residential Builder licence at $600 and up, Wisconsin requires Dwelling Contractor registration for permitted residential work
  • Two or three references — ideally from a year ago, not last week, so we can hear how the work aged

Straight answers

You're an independent contractor, not an employee. You set your own hours, bring your own tools and insurance, and take or decline each job.

We can't promise volume. How much work you see depends on what we sell in your area and what you're set up to do. Applying doesn't guarantee a single job.

There's no fee, ever. We don't charge contractors to join, and we don't sell leads. If anyone asks you to pay to be on a list, it isn't us.

The customer stays ours. We've quoted the job and we carry the relationship. You're paid by us for the work in the scope.
