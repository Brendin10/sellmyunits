Common questions

Who will contact me?

An independent, licensed real estate professional, lender, or investor who works with multifamily properties in your area. sellmyunits.com makes the introduction; the professional provides the service.

Does this cost anything?

No. The request and the introduction are free, and you're never obligated to work with anyone.

Why focus only on 2+ unit properties?

Multifamily deals turn on rents, expenses, cap rates, and financing rules that differ from single-family homes. Working with someone who handles these every day makes a real difference.

Can I get a value estimate without selling?

Yes. Many owners ask for a value and rent estimate to plan a refinance or a 1031 exchange, or just to know where they stand.

Can I sell a portfolio of properties?

Yes. Several rental houses, multiple buildings, or a mix of both. We'll connect you with someone who handles portfolio sales and can advise whether to sell as a package or separately.

Which states do you cover?

Indiana, Michigan and Wisconsin. Tell us where the property is and we'll match you with someone licensed in that state. Own in more than one of them? That's common, and it isn't a problem.
