Common questions

Who will contact me?

An independent, licensed real estate professional, lender, or investor who works with multifamily properties in your area. Forecast Plus makes the introduction; the professional provides the service.

Does this cost anything?

No. The request and the introduction are free, and you're never obligated to work with anyone.

Why focus only on 2+ unit properties?

Multifamily deals turn on rents, expenses, cap rates, and financing rules that differ from single-family homes. Working with someone who handles these every day makes a real difference.

Can I get a value estimate without selling?

Yes. Many owners ask for a value and rent estimate to plan a refinance or a 1031 exchange, or just to know where they stand.

Do you cover both Indiana and Illinois?

Yes, Northern Indiana and the Chicago metro. Tell us where the property is and we'll match you with someone licensed in that state.
